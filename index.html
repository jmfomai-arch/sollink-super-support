Before you apply: official rules

PALM workers may be able to claim super through a Departing Australia Superannuation Payment (DASP) after leaving Australia for the last time and when their Australian visa has expired or been cancelled, with no active Australian visa. Eligibility depends on individual circumstances; this app cannot guarantee approval.

• Official ATO DASP application
• PALM scheme: superannuation and tax
• PALM DASP application factsheet

You can prepare before leaving Australia, but generally cannot finish and submit until after departure and visa expiry/cancellation. ATO-held unclaimed super may still be claimable.

Suggested support stages

Request received → Checklist prepared → Worker applies officially → Awaiting ATO/fund → Outcome recorded

Claims on behalf of clients require specific ATO intermediary eligibility and authorisation. This prototype does not submit claims and does not represent SolLink as an authorised intermediary.

Official free support

PALM workers still in Australia may be able to access DASP Advisory Service support through the PALM support service. Workers who returned home can contact their local labour sending unit.

PALM support line: 1800 51 51 31
Email: palm@dewr.gov.au
